## ANTI-PATTERNS / GOTCHAS

- Do not instantiate repositories directly; route everything through `AnalysisService` via `get_service()` / `get_api_service()`.
- Datasources are built via `ClientContext` from [datasources/base.py](file:///home/zipper/Projects/football-analysis/src/football_analysis/datasources/base.py); do not construct adapters by hand.
- `devig.enabled` is `false` by default; `rolling_clv_brake` stays disabled until enough live bets exist.
